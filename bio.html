Bio

Academic Biography

I am an undergraduate student at the University of Southern California, where I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Applied and Computational Mathematics. My academic interests include artificial intelligence, machine learning, large language models, data-driven modeling, data engineering, operations modeling, and visual world models.

My research experience spans several areas of computational and data-driven inquiry. I have worked with large language models through supervised fine-tuning, Chain-of-Thought supervision, dataset preparation, and large-scale preprocessing workflows. I have also processed and analyzed large-scale datasets, including satellite imagery, geospatial layers, and time-series data, with a focus on structured feature extraction and data-driven modeling.

I am also interested in visual world models, operations modeling, and the use of data engineering methods to support applied research. My recent work includes probing world models for physical property discovery from visual data and building operational analysis tools for call center systems. More broadly, I am interested in computational approaches to scientific and interdisciplinary problems, especially where machine learning and careful data workflows can support analysis of complex systems.
