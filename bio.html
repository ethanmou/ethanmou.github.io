Bio

Academic Biography

I am an undergraduate student at the University of Southern California, where I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Applied and Computational Mathematics. My academic interests include artificial intelligence, machine learning, large language models, data-driven modeling, data engineering, operations modeling, and visual world models.

My research experience spans several areas of computational and data-driven inquiry. I have adapted large language models through LoRA-based supervised fine-tuning and stepwise solution supervision, improving MATH-500 accuracy from 60.0% to 70.3%. My work also includes large-scale data preprocessing, Lean4-based formal verification, and the analysis of satellite imagery, geospatial layers, and time-series data for environmental modeling and long-term land cover change.

My recent work includes applying INT8 weight-only quantization to a world model, reducing inference GPU memory usage by 33% and increasing decoding throughput by 15% versus a BF16 baseline. I also build virtual poking and 3D deformation-analysis pipelines, and have developed a discrete-event simulator and statistical inference workflow for SNAP call centers that identified queue-length approximation error in a fluid model. More broadly, I am interested in computational approaches to scientific and interdisciplinary problems, especially where machine learning and careful data workflows can support analysis of complex systems.
