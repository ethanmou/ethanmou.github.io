My research interests connect machine learning, data-driven modeling, data engineering, operations modeling, and visual understanding.

Physical Property Discovery from Motion and World Models

University of Southern California | Undergraduate Research Assistant | Feb 2026 - Present

At the University of Southern California, I conduct research on extracting object-level physical properties and structural information from motion using the PSI world model and SpelkeNet. My work includes virtual poking experiments in which I specify hold and movement constraints, then analyze the resulting sparse and dense optical flow fields. I also extend ideas from video-based vibrometry to investigate whether PSI-predicted motion under virtual interactions can reveal object stiffness, flexibility, and physical structure.

Discrete-Event Simulation for Call Center Operations

DSO Summer Scholars | Undergraduate Researcher | May 2026 - August 2026

As a DSO Summer Scholar, I developed a Python-based discrete-event simulation to model call center queueing, abandonment, redial behavior, and repeated caller attempts. I built an interactive Streamlit dashboard for parameter sweeps, attempt distribution visualization, and policy sensitivity analysis. I also designed a Monte Carlo evaluation framework to compare operational performance across three call centers and multiple policies.

GitHub: ethanmou/snap-stochastic-simulator

Improving Mathematical Reasoning in Large Language Models

Shanghai AI Laboratory | Research Intern | May 2025 - December 2025

At Shanghai AI Laboratory, I worked on improving the mathematical reasoning ability of large language models. I fine-tuned DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B using Chain-of-Thought supervision to improve multi-step mathematical reasoning. I also built and preprocessed large-scale datasets for model pretraining, including data cleaning, normalization, and formatting to support stable training runs, and investigated Lean4-based formal verification as a tool for improving LLM mathematical reasoning capabilities.

Large-Scale Data Processing and Land Cover Change Analysis

University of California Irvine | Undergraduate Research Assistant | November 2022 - November 2023

At the University of California Irvine, I processed and analyzed large-scale datasets, including satellite imagery, geospatial layers, and time-series data, to extract structured features for modeling. I used QGIS and remote sensing data to perform a 30-year land cover change analysis, and I used exploratory data analysis and correlation analysis to identify nonlinear relationships and regional patterns in high-dimensional data.
