World Model Inference and 3D Deformation Analysis

University of Southern California | Undergraduate Research Assistant | Feb 2026 - Present

At the University of Southern California, I apply model quantization to the PSI-0.5 world model's 8B-parameter autoregressive Transformer backbone to improve inference efficiency. I built an interactive Gradio virtual poking pipeline that combines monocular depth estimation and sparse motion prompts with PSI-predicted RGB, depth, and dense optical flow. I also reconstruct tracked 3D surface correspondences, estimate local deformation gradients using KNN neighborhood search and weighted least-squares fitting, and compute Green-Lagrange strain and SVD-based principal stretches to characterize local stretch, compression, and deformation anisotropy.

SNAP Call Center Simulation and Statistical Inference

DSO Summer Scholars | Undergraduate Researcher | May 2026 - Aug 2026

As a DSO Summer Scholar, I developed a Python discrete-event simulator for Missouri SNAP call centers, modeling queueing, caller abandonment, and repeat call attempts. I validated simulation averages against fluid-model predictions across three call-center settings and compared geometric maximum-likelihood estimates with fluid-flow estimates of caller attempt distributions, tracing a mismatch to congestion-estimation bias. A diagnostic queue-length substitution reduced parameter error by approximately 63% in that case. I also built a Streamlit dashboard for reproducible Monte Carlo experiments, parameter sweeps, and scenario comparisons of waiting times, abandonment, and caller outcomes.

LLM Mathematical Reasoning and Formal Verification

Shanghai AI Laboratory | Research Intern | May 2025 - December 2025

At Shanghai AI Laboratory, I fine-tuned DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B with chain-of-thought supervision for multi-step mathematical reasoning, incorporating intermediate reasoning steps into supervised training. I built preprocessing workflows for large-scale pretraining datasets, covering data cleaning, normalization, and format standardization to prepare consistent training inputs. I also explored Lean4-based formal verification as an approach to checking mathematical reasoning and improving the reliability of LLM-generated solutions.

Long-Term Land Cover Change and Geospatial Analysis

University of California, Irvine | Undergraduate Research Assistant | Nov 2022 - Nov 2023

At the University of California, Irvine, I processed satellite imagery, geospatial layers, and time-series data to extract structured features from large-scale datasets for environmental modeling. I conducted a 30-year land cover change analysis using QGIS and remote sensing data to examine long-term spatial change, and used exploratory data analysis and correlation analysis to investigate nonlinear relationships and regional patterns in high-dimensional data.
