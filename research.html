World Model Inference and 3D Deformation Analysis

University of Southern California | Undergraduate Research Assistant | Feb 2026 - Present

At the University of Southern California, I apply INT8 weight-only quantization to the attention and feedforward layers of PSI-0.5's 8B-parameter autoregressive Transformer backbone, reducing GPU memory usage during inference by 33% and increasing decoding throughput by 15% versus a BF16 baseline. I built an interactive Gradio virtual poking pipeline that combines monocular depth estimation and sparse motion prompts with PSI-predicted RGB, depth, and dense optical flow. I also reconstruct tracked 3D surface correspondences, estimate local deformation gradients using KNN neighborhood search and weighted least-squares fitting, and compute Green-Lagrange strain and SVD-based principal stretches to characterize local stretch, compression, and deformation anisotropy.

SNAP Call Center Simulation and Statistical Inference

DSO Summer Scholars | Undergraduate Researcher | May 2026 - Aug 2026

As a DSO Summer Scholar, I developed a Python discrete-event simulator for Missouri SNAP call centers, tracking caller histories across queueing, abandonment, service outcomes, and repeat attempts. I validated simulation averages against fluid-model predictions across three call-center configurations using Missouri SNAP parameters. By comparing maximum-likelihood and fluid-flow estimates of the geometric caller-attempt distribution parameter, I identified overestimated fluid-model queue lengths as a major source of disagreement. Substituting the simulated mean queue length into the fluid-flow estimator reduced parameter-estimation error by approximately 63% in the mismatch scenario. I also built a Streamlit dashboard for reproducible Monte Carlo experiments, parameter sweeps, and scenario comparisons of waiting times, abandonment, and caller outcomes.

LLM Mathematical Reasoning and Formal Verification

Shanghai AI Laboratory | Research Intern | May 2025 - December 2025

At Shanghai AI Laboratory, I adapted DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B through supervised fine-tuning with LoRA and stepwise solution supervision, training adapter parameters while freezing the base model weights. This increased MATH-500 accuracy from 60.0% to 70.3%. I built preprocessing workflows for large-scale pretraining datasets, covering data cleaning, normalization, and format standardization to prepare consistent training inputs. I also explored Lean4-based formal verification as an approach to checking mathematical reasoning and improving the reliability of LLM-generated solutions.
